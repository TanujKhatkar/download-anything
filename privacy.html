Download Anything

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Download Anything is a Chrome extension that saves videos from links you provide. This page explains exactly what happens to your data — in plain language.

The short version: nothing is collected, nothing is uploaded, and no server is involved. Everything happens on your own computer.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising and no user accounts. There is no sign-up and no login.

2. What is stored on your device

A single setting: whether “Use browser cookies” is switched on. It is saved locally using chrome.storage.local, it never leaves your device, and it is never synced to any account. No other data is stored.

3. How your links are handled

Links you paste are sent to a small download engine running on your own computer at 127.0.0.1 (a loopback address, which cannot reach the internet as a server). They are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are not logged.

The engine deletes its temporary files automatically when a download finishes, and in any case within one hour.

4. Clipboard

The clipboard is read only at the moment you click the Paste button, and only to fill the link field. It is never read in the background, never stored, and never transmitted.

5. Browser cookies (optional, off by default)

If you switch on “Use browser cookies”, the local engine reads your existing browser session cookies so it can open posts that require you to be logged in — for example private or age-restricted videos you already have access to.

This happens entirely on your computer. The cookies are not copied, exported, stored or transmitted by the developer. Turning the setting off stops it immediately.

6. Downloads

Finished files are saved to your normal Downloads folder by Chrome's own download manager. The extension has no access to your existing files and does not read your download history.

7. Page content and permissions

  • activeTab — reads the address of the tab you are on, only after you click the icon, so your current video link can be filled in.
  • scripting — an optional button scans the current tab for a plain video file already on the page. It runs only after you click it.
  • host permission for 127.0.0.1:8765 — this is the local engine on your machine, not a remote service.

8. Third parties

No data is sold, rented, shared or transferred to anyone. There are no third-party SDKs, fonts, scripts or trackers on this site or in the extension.

9. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

10. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by the developer, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You can remove the single local setting at any time by uninstalling the extension.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the project repository or email tanujkhatkar18@gmail.com.

Note on third-party content: Download Anything is a neutral tool. Please use it only for content you own or have the right to save, and respect each site's terms of service and copyright law.
